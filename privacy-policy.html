Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 | Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Welcome to Kharu Content Studio ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our personal automation application ("Kharu Content Studio") interacts with third-party platforms including the Pinterest API.

1. Scope and Purpose

Kharu Content Studio is an automated personal content creator used solely by its owner (Aparna Janwalkar) to publish original creative artworks, visual carousels, and stories of "Kharuchi Jadui Dunia" to dedicated social channels including Pinterest.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our application does not collect, store, sell, or process any personal data from general users, visitors, or Pinners. The application only uses authenticated OAuth 2.0 access tokens to:

  • Create and organize boards under our authenticated Pinterest profile.
  • Publish image pins and carousel stories created by our studio.
  • Read publishing status to verify successful uploads.

3. Third-Party Services (Pinterest API)

Our integration strictly follows the Pinterest Developer Terms and Guidelines. We do not share any credentials, access tokens, or account analytics with third parties.

4. Data Retention and Security

Access tokens are stored securely in encrypted local environment variables and are never transmitted to outside servers. No user tracking cookies or third-party analytical trackers are used on this site.
